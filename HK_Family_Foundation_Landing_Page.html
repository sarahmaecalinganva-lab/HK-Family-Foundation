Our Founder’s Story

The Battle of 2010: a battle that changed my life forever 💕

Some battles leave scars. Others change the way you see the world forever. My battle with breast cancer in 2010 did both. And while I would never wish that journey on anyone, I can say with my whole heart that it helped shape the woman I am today.

Losing my hair was the least of my worries. My babies were just 5 and 7 years old. We had just moved to a new city for my job, and everything around us was unfamiliar. I was trying to build a new life for my family while quietly fighting for my own.

I did everything I could to keep life normal for my children. I wanted them to feel safe, happy, and loved, even on the days when I could barely find the strength to eat or drink a glass of water.

I remember those days like they were yesterday. The exhaustion. The fear. The uncertainty. The moments when I wondered how much more my body could take.

But what I remember most is God’s faithfulness. 🙏🏼

He carried me through days I didn’t think I could get through. He gave me strength when I had none left. And He blessed me with something I will never take for granted: a second chance at life.

Cancer taught me lessons I could never have learned any other way.

It taught me that none of us are invincible. That tomorrow is never promised. That the little things we rush through every day are often the very things that matter most.

  • The conversations.
  • The hugs.
  • The laughter around the dinner table.
  • The people who show up.
  • The time we get with those we love.

Cancer taught me not just how precious life is, but how important it is to truly LIVE it.

Was it hard? Absolutely.
Were there days that broke me? More than I can count.
Would I change my journey? Not a chance.

Because somewhere in the middle of that battle, God was preparing me for a purpose much bigger than myself.

I believe with everything in me that God gave me a second chance for a reason. And I made Him a promise that I intend to keep for the rest of my life.

“As long as I have breath in my lungs, I will do everything I can to make a difference in the lives of others.”

I will stand beside those fighting cancer. I will help families who are struggling through the unimaginable. I will encourage people to find their strength, recognize their worth, and walk boldly in the purpose God created them for.

Because surviving was never going to be the end of my story. It was the beginning of my mission. 💕

To those who are fighting right now…

I wish I could wrap my arms around every single one of you.

I know you’re tired. I know there are days when being strong feels impossible. I know there are moments when you smile for everyone around you while quietly falling apart inside.

But please hear me when I say this: you have more strength inside you than you realize.

And on the days when you feel like you have nothing left, hold on. Sometimes all you can do is get through the next hour, and that’s enough.

Anyone who knows me has heard me say:

“You can get down, but you CAN’T stay down!”

And I mean that with all my heart.

If you need someone to listen, someone to pray with you, a shoulder to cry on, or simply a hug from someone who understands, I am here. You never have to pretend to be strong with me. 💕

To the beautiful souls we have lost… 🕊️

You are not forgotten. Not today. Not tomorrow. Not ever.

We know how hard you fought. We remember your courage, your smiles, your love, and the beautiful memories you left behind.

I find comfort in believing you are resting peacefully in the arms of our Heavenly Father, free from pain and surrounded by a love greater than we can imagine.

And if heaven allows, send us a little sign every now and then to remind us just how beautiful it is up there.

Until we meet again. 💕🙏🏼

To the caregivers, families, friends, and loved ones who fight alongside us…

You are the quiet heroes of this journey.

We know it isn’t easy. We know there are sleepless nights, hidden tears, overwhelming fears, and moments when you feel completely helpless.

We see your sacrifices. We feel your love. And even when we don’t have the words or the energy to say it, please know how deeply grateful we are.

You give us reasons to smile on our hardest days. You remind us what we’re fighting for. You help carry us when we can no longer carry ourselves.

Thank you for showing up. Thank you for staying. Thank you for loving us through the hardest moments of our lives. We couldn’t do it without you.

Looking back, sixteen years later

Today, as I look back on 2010, I don’t just see a woman who battled cancer.

I see a young mother who desperately wanted more time with her babies.

I see a family whose faith was tested.

I see a woman who learned that strength doesn’t mean never falling apart. Sometimes strength is simply trusting God enough to keep going.

And today, I see a woman who has been blessed with 16 more years of memories, milestones, laughter, love, and opportunities to make a difference.

Sixteen years I never want to take for granted.

If my journey has taught me anything, it’s this:

  • Don’t wait for a diagnosis to appreciate your life.
  • Don’t wait for a loss to tell someone you love them.
  • Don’t wait for the perfect moment to chase the purpose God has placed on your heart.

Love deeply. Forgive freely. Make the memories. Take the pictures. Have the conversations. And never underestimate the difference you can make in someone else’s life.

God didn’t promise us a life without storms, but I am living proof that He can carry us through them.

My battle was in 2010, but my purpose continues every single day.

And I pray that when my time on this earth is done, I will have honored the second chance He gave me by helping others find hope, strength, and purpose in their own journeys.

Thank You, Jesus, for the gift of more time. I promise not to waste it. 💕🙏🏼

To every warrior, every survivor, every caregiver, and every angel watching over us: this is for you.

💕 Keep fighting. Keep believing. Keep living. Your story matters. 💕
